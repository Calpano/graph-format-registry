= SMILES
:toc:

A compact line-notation molecular-graph format. Atoms are written as element
symbols (nodes) and bonds as the implicit or explicit characters between them
(edges); ring-closure digits encode cycles. Per-atom attributes such as charge
and isotope go inside bracketed atoms, and bond order is a typed edge
(`-`, `=`, `#`, `:`).
