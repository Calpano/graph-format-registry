= Notation3 (N3)
:toc:

A text syntax that extends RDF with rules, formulae (quoted graphs) and
built-in functions. Resources are nodes and triples are directed edges typed by
their predicate. link:../turtle/turtle.adoc[Turtle] is a subset of N3.

== Features

[source]
----
format:notation3 ..supports.. multiple-graphs-per-document ,,
..status.. partial
Formulae are quoted graphs embedded as terms, not named graphs
,,
format:notation3 ..supports.. nodes ,, Resources (IRIs and blank nodes)
format:notation3 ..lacks.. undirected-edges
format:notation3 ..supports.. directed-edges ,, Triples are directed subject to object
format:notation3 ..lacks.. hyperedges
format:notation3 ..lacks.. mixed-directionality-edges
format:notation3 ..supports.. edges-on-edges ,,
..status.. partial
Via reification or a quoted single-triple formula
,,
format:notation3 ..supports.. nested-graphs-in-nodes ,, Formulae { ... } may be subject or object of a triple
format:notation3 ..supports.. nested-graphs-in-edges ,,
..status.. partial
Formulae may appear as predicate terms
,,
format:notation3 ..supports.. nested-graphs-in-graphs ,,
..status.. partial
Formulae may be nested inside other formulae
,,
format:notation3 ..lacks.. node-labels ,, No native label slot; rdfs:label is data, not syntax
format:notation3 ..supports.. edge-labels ,, Predicate IRI names the edge
format:notation3 ..supports.. attributes-on-nodes ,, Literal-valued properties on resources
format:notation3 ..lacks.. attributes-on-edges ,, Edge is the predicate; no per-triple attributes except via reification
format:notation3 ..lacks.. attributes-on-graphs
format:notation3 ..supports.. typed-edges ,, Every triple is typed by its predicate
----
