= Mermaid Architecture
:toc:

The architecture type of link:mermaid.adoc[Mermaid], opened by the
`architecture-beta` keyword. Services, groups and junctions are nodes; a service
or group may sit `in` a parent group (containment); connections join services,
optionally with a directed arrow.
